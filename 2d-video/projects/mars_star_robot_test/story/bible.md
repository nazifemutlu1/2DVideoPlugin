# Mars Star Robot — story bible

## Logline
A small, curious teal service robot stands alone on the surface of Mars. In its cupped hands it carries one tiny golden star, and for a few quiet seconds the dead planet feels warm.

## Cast
**TIB (Tin Inch Bot)** — a knee-high field survey robot, original design for this project.
- Rounded square head with a dark glass face, two separate aqua eye lights, two copper ear pods and a low copper crest.
- Pear-shaped brushed teal chassis, copper collar, small back power pack and a visible cable.
- Stubby articulated legs with ball hips, hinge knees, hydraulic shin rods and oversized soft boots.
- Palette: oxidized teal `#2f6a68`, copper `#c07a49`, dark joint metal, aqua eyes `#8cf4e3`.
- Personality: quiet, curious, careful. It holds the star as if it might break.
- Readable silhouette: big square-ish head, pear body, big boots, star held low in front of the chest.

## Location
Mars at late dusk. Deep indigo / plum sky with a dust-warmed horizon, faint stars and Phobos. Layered mesas and cliffs recede in atmospheric haze; the near ground is rust red with craters, ejecta and scattered rocks. Thin dust drifts laterally in the wind. The only warm practical light is the star itself.

## Palette and light
- Sky: indigo to dusty rose, horizon glow `#c87a4f`.
- Ground: `#6b342c` to `#351a1a`.
- Ambient: cool blue-violet sky light, weak.
- Key: the star — warm gold `#ffd27a`; it under-lights the robot's chin, chest and hands, and casts a soft pool at its feet.
- Rim: cool cyan sky rim on the top/back edges, warm star rim on the front/bottom edges.
- Grade: engine `dusk` style (mauve shadows, amber light, 2:1 letterbox, grain, vignette).

## Story beats
1. **0.0–2.6 s — Reveal.** Wide, low camera. Mars stretches to layered cliffs; TIB is a small figure on the right third, head bowed to the dim star. The camera eases in; dust crosses frame. The head begins to lift.
2. **2.6–5.0 s — Lift.** Medium shot. TIB raises the star from its belly to its chest. The light grows, copper and teal warm up, eyes widen, small mouth opens. A small body lift and head tilt sell the effort and wonder.
3. **5.0–7.4 s — Wonder.** Close-up. Star at full soft brilliance, face gilded from below. Eyes soften into a happy squint, mouth curls into a quiet smile, the camera settles. Final held composition, then a gentle fade.

## Camera
- Slow push-ins only; no static frames.
- Parallax across five depth layers (sky, far mesas, ridge, cliffs, ground/foreground).
- Shot 3 drifts to a near-rest final composition with the star on the lower-left third and the face on the upper-right third.
- 24 fps, 1280×720, H.264, 7.4 s.
