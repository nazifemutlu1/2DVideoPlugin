# Paper Balloon — directing bible

## Logline and tone
A tiny paper kid refuses to let go of an enormous paper balloon. It lifts him out of the street, over the rooftops and above the city; fear turns into acceptance. Tone: tactile, slightly comic, then airy and wondrous. Silent, 12 s, 9:16.

## One thread
- Who acts: a small paper puppet with a mustard hat and scarf.
- What changes: grip → lift-off → letting the height become play.
- What it proves: the thing that was too strong to hold becomes a way to fly.
- Why watch: the scale gap between a tiny hero and a giant balloon keeps growing, and the city keeps falling away.

## Cast
**Pip** — original paper-cutout kid, not a stick figure. Layered cream paper head with a cut fringe, teal overalls over a cream shirt, mustard paper cap + mustard scarf (the single signature colour), brass brads at shoulders, elbows, hips, knees and wrists. Large head, small body, oversized shoes. Geometry: irregular polygon head, trapezoid torso, tapered paper-strip limbs, rolled-paper grip handle. Poses: brace, strain, yank, dangle, flail, pull-up, perch, cheer. Faces: determined, strain, scared, awe, joy. Blink ~3.1 s.

## Palette and light
Paper stock palette: deep teal sky (#0f2b38 → #1f5562), warm apricot horizon (#f0b275), cream paper (#f1e2c8), coral balloon (#e05a43) with rose/cream gores, mustard accents (#e8b44c), dark ink shadows. One key from upper right; every cut piece has a soft down-left offset shadow, a lit cut edge and a paper-fibre texture. City is teal/ink with cream roofs and mustard windows. The coral balloon is the only large saturated warm object besides the sun.

## Locations / layers
**Paper street** — vertical canyon at dusk. Sky/light .08; far skyline .25; mid blocks .42; near facades + shop details .68; pavement .9; hero + balloon 1.0; blurred bunting, awning and lamp post 1.5–1.75. Key props: scalloped awning, hanging balloon-shop sign, crate, bollard, cracked pavement. GY = 0.78 H. Balloon cropped above frame in s001.

**Paper aerial** — cut-paper city diorama seen from above, big sky. Sky/sun .08–.12; far city .22; mid city .38; near rooftops/streets/park/canal .62; balloon shadow .5; hero + balloon 1.0; foreground cloud 1.45. Buildings are extruded paper blocks with roof details, warm cut windows, water towers, trees, tiny cars, a canal and two bridges. s004 city begins around 0.56 H; s005 city begins around 0.69 H.

## Motifs
- The twine: taut in s001–s004, curling free below the balloon in s005; a small paper tag with an arrow-cut notch swings on it.
- The mustard scarf: always lags the body; it is the wind indicator and the emotional tell.
- The cut edge: every shape has one lit edge and one shadowed edge; no gradients on the hero.
- Scale reveal: the balloon starts cropped, becomes full, then becomes a small crown under the kid.

## Episode / beat cards
### Beat 1 — Hold (0–4.55)
Meaning: the balloon is stronger than the kid. Action: brace, slide, strain; insert of hands/knot/shoe. Camera: low-angle vertical push-in (s001), handheld insert with a tiny tilt (s002). Text: none. Transition: cut on the upward jerk. Risk: hands must visibly connect to the handle and twine.

### Beat 2 — Lift-off (4.55–7.75)
Meaning: the city lets go before he does. Action: shoes leave pavement, flail, then steady. Camera: vertical tracking with lag; facades drop in parallax; end cloud wipe. Optics: layer blur + ghost copies (imitation only). Transition: match cut through the foreground cloud.

### Beat 3 — Above the city (7.75–12.0)
Meaning: fear becomes play. Action: dangle, look down, pull knees up; cut to perched on the crown, arms up. Camera: crane back over the diorama (s004); close-to-wide reveal (s005). Transition: fade 0.3 in, fade to black out. Final image: coral paper sun-balloon, tiny kid on top, long twine, city below.

## Variants considered (selection)
**Beat 1:** A low-angle street canyon + insert (chosen: clearest weight/contact in 9:16) / B top-down tension diagram (too abstract) / C over-the-balloon shoulder (hides face) / D lateral drag along shopfront (weaker vertical use).
**Beat 2:** A vertical tracking with falling facades + cloud wipe (chosen: readable cause and honest 2.5D) / B rooftop shadow (poetic, loses contact) / C fake 3D orbit (would expose 2D layers) / D string snap-and-catch (too much story for 3 s).
**Beat 3:** A aerial diorama + crown close + pull-out (chosen: resolves the arc and gives a final image) / B flat map (breaks the world) / C bird escort (adds characters that compete) / D one-handed slip (tension without resolution).

## Risk probe
Primary risk: the lift-off must read as acceleration with layered parallax, not as a flat pan. Probe with s003 stills at 4.7/5.4/6.6/7.5 and a dense frame strip of 4.55–7.75 before the full render. Secondary risk: the paper kid must read at 300 px tall; check face and hand silhouette at sheet scale.

## Sound
None by brief. No music, no SFX, no captions. This is a silent render test.
